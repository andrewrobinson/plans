# Buying guide: the cupboard options

How to describe each option when buying ready-made cabinets. The options match the 3D model in [room_3d.html](room_3d.html), keys 1–5.

Ready-made cabinets are almost always rectangular boxes, so each option is given as its overall shape, then the standard units that make it up.

## Reading the sizes

- **W × D × H:** W is the width of the door face, D the depth from front to back, H the height. All in mm.
- **Hinge side** is as you stand facing the door.
- **Depth:** all units are **500 deep**. Many ready-made kitchen and wardrobe units are 560–600 deep, so 500 may need a shallower range or cut-down carcasses.
- **Height:** the ceiling is **2400**. Ready-made tall units usually come shorter, around 2000–2300, plus a filler or scribe panel up to the ceiling.
- **Room:** 3838 wide × 5717 deep. The bench is 500 deep and 780 high, along the back wall.

## Option 1: Open shelves in the corner

**Shape:** a 500 × 1200 rectangle in plan against the left wall, full height. The bench (3338, from x 500) butts against its side.

| Unit | W × D × H | Front faces | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tall cupboard | 700 × 500 × 2400 | into the room | One door 694 × 2394, **left-hinged** |
| Open shelf unit | 500 × 500 × 1620 | into the room, above the bench | Sits at 780. Back panel against the left wall, 3 shelves |
| Corner void | 500 × 500 × 780 | none | Under the shelf unit, behind the bench end |

**Not standard:** reaching the corner void through the tall door needs the tall unit's side panel left off below 780. With off-the-shelf units, that corner is a dead space.

## Option 3: Upper cupboard on the bench, with a door

**Shape:** an **L** made of the tall cupboard on the left wall (full height) and an upper cupboard along the back wall above the bench (780–2400). Both inside legs are 700, and the outside is 1200 × 1200. The bench stays 3338 from x 500.

| Unit | W × D × H | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tall cupboard | 700 × 500 × 2400 | One door, **left-hinged** |
| Upper **blind-corner** cupboard | 1200 × 500 × 1620 | Sits on the bench at 780. Its left 500 is the corner, behind the tall cupboard. Door 676 × 1614 on the right 700, **right-hinged** |
| Corner void | 500 × 500 × 780 | As option 1 |

"Blind corner" is the standard trade term for a cabinet whose end disappears into a corner. Ask for that.

## Option 5: Full-height L, bench from 1200

**Shape:** a **full-height L**, floor to ceiling on both legs, with both inside legs 700 and the outside 1200 × 1200. The bench is shortened to **2638**, from x 1200.

You can buy it as either:

- **A corner wardrobe:** an "L-shaped corner wardrobe, 1200 × 1200 outside, 500 deep, 2400 high, one 700 door on each inside face". Or
- **Two tall units:**

| Unit | W × D × H | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tall **blind-corner** unit, along the back wall | 1200 × 500 × 2400 | Left 500 is the corner. Door 676 × 2394 on the right 700, **right-hinged** |
| Tall unit, along the left wall | 700 × 500 × 2400 | Door 694 × 2394, **left-hinged**. It stands in front of the blind corner |

This is the most standard of the five. There's no void under a bench, and the corner is reached through the back-wall unit's door.

## All options

- **Corner filler:** the two doors that meet at the inside corner swing into the same space. If you buy ready-made, expect to fit a corner filler strip (about 50 mm) so the doors and handles clear each other.
- **Door sizes** above allow a 3 mm gap round each door. A supplier's doors may be slightly different, so check against their gap allowances.
- **Room door:** 760 × 2048, hinged at 1380 from the back wall on the left wall, opening into the room. None of the options get in its way when it's opened to 90°.
