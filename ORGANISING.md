# Organising the flat and the garage

How to sort tools, electrical bits, spares and everyday clutter, and how to
decide what lives in the garage and what stays in the flat.

## How to work through it

- **One category at a time, not one room at a time.** Gather the whole
  category from everywhere (every allen key, every cable), sort it using the
  charts below, decide where each thing goes, then put it away.
- **One home for everything.** Each category gets one labelled box, drawer
  or shelf. Clear boxes, labelled with the category names below.
- **Small parts in organiser cases** (fixings, crimp terminals, fuses).
- **Garage wall:** a pegboard or wall rail for the hand tools you use most,
  so they don't go back into piles.

## Garage or flat: the rule

Ask these questions in order and stop at the first "yes":

1. Is it for a vehicle (motorbike, car, 12V)? → **Garage**
2. Is it messy, oily, flammable, heavy or loud? → **Garage**
3. Is it a power tool or a woodwork/metalwork tool? → **Garage**
4. Is it for the flat's own upkeep, or used indoors at least every month or
   two? → **Flat kit**
5. Is it a spare part for something in the flat (kitchen, Ikea)? →
   **Flat spares box**
6. Is it a duplicate? → Keep the best one in the best place. A second copy
   only stays if both places really need one.
7. Unused for 2 years and cheap to replace? → **Let it go**

Phone chargers, laptop leads and household cables always stay indoors.

## The flat kit (one box, one shelf)

- Screwdrivers: PZ1, PZ2, small flat, electrical terminal
- Small hammer, tape measure, spirit level, craft knife
- Pliers, side cutters, adjustable spanner
- One metric allen key set
- Torch, voltage tester
- Picture hooks, wall plugs and screws, cable ties, tape
- Spare plug fuses: 3A, 5A, 13A

Everything else lives in the garage. The cordless drill lives where it's
used most and visits the other place.

## The flat spares box (kitchen and Ikea)

Hinges, mounting plates, shelf pins, cam locks and bolts, dowels, handles
and their screws, plinth clips, leftover Ikea bags. Small bags, labelled by
what they're for ("kitchen hinge", "wardrobe"), not by what they are.

```
│   ├── Gripping ..... pliers, mole grips, clamps, vice
│   ├── Striking ..... hammers, mallets, punches
│   ├── Cutting ...... knives, snips, files

├── Power tools ...... drill/driver, impact driver, grinder, jigsaw, sander, multitool
│   └── Batteries & chargers (kept together, one shelf)
├── Vehicle (garage)
│   ├── Lifting ...... paddock stands, axle stands, jack
│   ├── Bike-specific  chain breaker/riveter, tyre levers, spark plug sockets, brake bleeding kit
│   ├── Servicing .... oil filter wrench, drain pan, funnels, pressure gauge, compressor
│   └── Fluids ....... oils, grease, chain lube, brake fluid, cleaners (sealed bin, off the floor)
├── Woodwork (garage)  saws, chisels, plane, clamps, dowel jig, sanding, glue
├── Metalwork (garage) hacksaw, files, taps & dies, HSS bits, centre punch, cutting fluid
├── Fixings & hardware
│   ├── Metric nuts/bolts/washers (garage, organiser case by size)
│   ├── Screws, wall plugs, nails (a small selection in the flat kit, bulk in the garage)
│   └── Furniture hardware → flat spares box
├── Consumables ...... tape, cable ties, abrasives, blades, glue, rags
└── Safety ........... glasses, gloves, ear defenders, dust masks (next to the power tools)
```

```
│   └── Accessories ..... USB/12V sockets, loom tape, convoluted tube, P-clips

│                               choc block, junction boxes, socket/switch fittings

    ├── Chargers ........ phone, laptop, watch: each with its lead, bagged or tied
```

**Mystery adapters and cables:** test each one and label it with its
device. Any you can't name go in a box dated today. Whatever's still in the
box after 3 months goes to electrical (WEEE) recycling.

## The things that end up everywhere

- **Allen keys:** gather every one in one sweep. Keep one good metric set
  (1.5–10 mm) in the flat kit and a ball-end or T-handle set in the garage.
  Keep a few loose 4 mm and 5 mm keys (the usual Ikea sizes) in the flat
  spares box and recycle the rest as scrap metal.
- **Glasses:** one home for each pair, where it's used: a tray on the desk,
  the bedside, by the door. Glasses off means into the tray.
- **Desk papers:** three trays: *To do*, *To file* and *To shred/recycle*.
  Clear *To file* weekly, scanning what you can so the paper goes. Nothing
  else lives flat on the desk.
