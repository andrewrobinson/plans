# Howdens' plan (read 22 Oct 2026)

Howdens' kitchen plan for the flat: Cad Plan No 26031629616, designed by
Matthew Ayres on 23/4/26, range Clerkenwell Gloss White. Photos in
`images/custom_island/` (`howdens_plan.jpg`, and the whole sheet, still to
save as `howdens_plan_full.jpg`). Sizes are Howdens' unit widths in mm;
positions are scaled off a photo, so only to about ±10%.

The plan is drawn turned: its bottom is the model's left wall (x = 0, the
door and kitchen recess), its left the back wall (y = 0, the TV wall), its
top the window wall and its right the front wall.

## Where the model differs from the plan

The room itself agrees, to what a photo shows: the kitchen recess about
1070 deep (measured 995), the return wall about y 2300 (measured 2386),
the door where it is, the fridge tower 600 + 2 × 16 = 632 (measured 630),
the sink in the corner run where the model has it, and the back wall's
tall units 3764 across the room's 3838. The plan draws the desk recess
about 1900 across; the measured 1623 stands.

The differences, left as they are (the user's, 22 Oct 2026: the custom
island's sizes are adjustable, so they don't matter):

1. **The island's carcass.** The handwritten 2400 x 950 over a "1600
   carcass" leaves an 800 overhang; the model starts at 400 (a 2000
   carcass), with an **overhang** slider from 400 to 800.
2. **Its place.** The plan draws it as a peninsula against the front
   wall (the model's **Long side**); the model starts it out in the room
   where TORNVIKEN stands.
3. **Its window side.** The plan has a 1000 HL there (a drawer over a
   door); the model has drawers both sides, as the user described it.
4. **The kitchen's worktop.** The plan's is grey oak effect; the model
   draws it light stone (`#CFC3B0`, estimated). The custom island's is
   grey oak effect.

Not in the model yet: the back wall's larders and highline units, the
units on the front wall by the desk recess, and the shelf above the sink.
