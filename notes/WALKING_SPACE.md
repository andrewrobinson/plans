# Walking space round the bed

Asked for 26 Oct 2026: a mode that shows the walk-through dimensions in the
bedroom, each coloured by whether it's a problem. Built as
`model/walking.js`, switched by **Show walking space** in Bedroom options'
Cupboard tab (not saved in the address).

## The user's rules

- A person walks round the bed **forwards, never sideways**: 600 clear.
- The bed stays where the user has it, 680 from the window wall. Its window
  side, **545** to the radiator, is tight but adequate.
- Today's dark bookcase on the chimney breast (290 deep) leaves **586** round
  the bed's foot, which works. The worry: a cupboard there would be worse.
- The bed has its bedside tables: the Knarrevik on the door side, the
  Kvistbro on the window side. The bedroom door is kept open, against them.
- **The bedroom door must be able to swing shut.** A hard limit.

## Agreed (the user's answers)

- Gaps: round the bed (its sides and foot), from the door to the bed, and in
  front of each of the new cupboard's doors.
- Colours: green 600 or more, orange 460 to 599 (forwards, but tight), red
  under 460 (sideways, or not at all). A cupboard door is red if it can't open
  to 90°, orange if it opens but leaves under 600 to stand at.
- Shown in plan and 3D, as dimensions on the floor, with a line of text;
  the switch at the top of Bedroom options, above its tabs.
- The bedroom door is measured as the user keeps it: opened as far as it
  goes, which is against the Knarrevik (the model stops it at a bedside
  table against the hallway wall). Every cupboard door is measured shut.
  The bedroom door's swing back to shut must be clear (red if not).
- Designer 1 to 5's bedroom door swings right, as the user's does.
